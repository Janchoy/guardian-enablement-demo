Governance & Risk Framework

Every FPRS AI tool operates within a tiered risk framework — defining human-in-the-loop requirements, data handling standards, and escalation paths. Governance enables adoption.

3
Risk Tiers
100%
Tools Rated
M365
Data Boundary
● Low Risk — Tier 1: Assist
AI drafts. Human acts.
No automated decisions or external output. Examples: meeting summaries, draft emails, document Q&A.
✦ Human reviews all output before use
✦ No PII required in prompt
✦ M365 data boundary enforced
✦ Standard Copilot audit logging
◐ Medium Risk — Tier 2: Augment
AI generates. Human approves.
AI outputs feed a decision process. Named human approver required before any action. Examples: RFP drafts, new business checklists.
✦ Named human approver in workflow
✦ PII handling per Guardian data policy
✦ Output versioning and audit trail
✦ Quarterly prompt accuracy review
▲ High Risk — Tier 3: Advise
AI advises. Compliance owns.
Touches regulatory, legal, or claims decision content. Compliance sign-off required. No AI-only output ever reaches a claimant.
✦ Compliance team sign-off required
✦ Legal review of all prompt templates
✦ Hard escalation path — no AI-only output
✦ Monthly model performance review

Program Rollout Phases

A structured 3-phase approach from foundation to scale — building sustainable AI fluency without central bottlenecks.

1

Foundation — Identify & Equip (Q1–Q2)

Recruit 1–2 champions per business unit. Deliver Copilot fundamentals training, provide prompt library access, establish feedback loop. Define champion charter and recognition structure.

2

Activation — Build & Share (Q2–Q3)

Champions build their first unit-specific prompts and simple agents. Monthly showcase sessions share wins. Central team publishes adoption dashboards and identifies friction points. Prompt library grows from champion contributions.

3

Scale — Sustain & Govern (Q3+)

Champions evolve into embedded AI advisors for their teams. Governance framework ensures quality and compliance. Peer-to-peer learning reduces dependence on central AI team.
